### Model

```rust
}
```

The stored Discover taste of one subject (`DeriveEntityModel`): what the
subject's books, listening and wishes say they like, computed by the
candidate fetch so a ranking reads it instead of the whole library.

**Fields**

- `subject_key`: Discover subject (`operator`, `user:<id>`, `portal:<id>`); one taste each.
- `taste_json`: The taste as JSON (`bookclerk_discover::StoredTaste`).
- `seed_scope`: Which seeds the taste comes from: `own` or `household`.
- `computed_at`: RFC 3339 timestamp when the taste was computed.
