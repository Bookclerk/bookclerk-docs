Row shape for one member's rule. A member with no row may connect.

**Fields**

- `user_id`: The member (`users.id`).
- `may_connect`: `1` when the member may connect store accounts on this instance,
  `0` when the operator refused it.
- `updated_at`: RFC 3339 time the rule was last set.
