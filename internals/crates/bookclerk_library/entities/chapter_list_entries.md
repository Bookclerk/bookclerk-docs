Row shape for one chapter of a stored list.

**Fields**

- `digest`: The list's digest (`chapter_lists.digest`).
- `position`: The chapter's place in the list, from 0.
- `start_ms`: Start, in milliseconds from the start of the edition's timeline.
- `depth`: Nesting depth; 0 for a top-level chapter.
- `title`: Chapter title; never empty.
